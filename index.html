Luanda, Angola

Angola is a beautiful country in Southern Africa, known for its rich culture, music, beautiful beaches, and large oil production. The country is one of Africa's top oil-producing nations, and Cabinda, an exclave province bordered by the Republic of Congo and the Democratic Republic of Congo contributes greatly to Angola's oil wealth. Angola is also blessed with the Atlantic Ocean, beautiful landscapes, and warm hospitality. A popular local saying is “Angola Kuya,” which means “Angola is sweet,” reflecting the joy, beauty, and lively spirit of the country and its people.
