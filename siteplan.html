Vacuum Flask Website Site Plan

Site Name

Bless Thermal And Vacuum Flask Store

Site Purpose

This website will present premium insulated food flasks, water flasks for children, workers, students, and families. Customers can learn about sizes, materials, benefits, and ordering information.

Target Audience

Parents, workers, students, and families looking for quality food containers.

Color Scheme

Deep Emerald Green — headings and navigation

Soft Gold — buttons and highlights

Cream White — background

Dark Gray — body text

Typography

Inter — headings and body text

Reason for Selection

I selected this website subject because I recently started a small business involving vacuum flasks, food flasks, and water flasks. This project will allow me to build a website connected to my real business interests while improving my web development skills.
