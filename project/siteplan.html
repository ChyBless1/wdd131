Vacuum Flask Website Site Plan

Site Name

Bless Thermal And Vacuum Flask Store

I chose this name because the website is connected to my small business, which focuses on selling quality thermal food flasks, vacuum flasks, and food-grade containers. The name is simple, clear, and easy for customers to understand.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to present food-grade thermal food flasks, vacuum flasks, and related containers for children, workers, students, and families. The site will help customers learn about product sizes, materials, quality levels, health benefits, and ordering information.

The website will also support the idea that health is wealth by promoting BPA-free and stainless steel products that help people store food and drinks safely.

Scenarios

  • Which vacuum flask keeps food or drinks hot or cold for many hours?
  • Which food flask is BPA-free, food-grade, and safe for children and families?
  • What quality options and price ranges are available for my budget?

Color Scheme

Deep Green — used for headings, navigation, and footer because it represents health, safety, and trust.

Cream White — used for the page background to create a clean and soft look.

Dark Gray — used for body text because it is easy to read and looks professional.

Soft Gold — used for borders, buttons, and small highlights to give the site a premium and classy feeling.

Typography

Poppins — headings, titles, and navigation links.

Inter — body text, paragraphs, and general website content.
